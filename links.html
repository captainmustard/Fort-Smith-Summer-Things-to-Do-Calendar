Fort Smith, Arkansas

Things to Do

A directory of kid-friendly and family-friendly spots, attractions, and resources around Fort Smith.

← Back to the calendar

Parks & Recreation

  • Fort Smith Parks & Recreation
    City parks, splash pads, pools, trails, and riverfront facilities.
  • Ben Geren Regional Park
    1,300+ acre county park with sports fields, disc golf, trails, waterpark, and golf course.
  • Parrot Island Waterpark
    Wave pool, slides, lazy river, and kids' area. Open Memorial Day through Labor Day.
  • Riverfront Skate & Bike Park
    12,000 sq ft outdoor park with pump tracks, vertical wall, and mountain bike playground.
  • Janet Huckabee Arkansas River Valley Nature Center
    170-acre facility with lake, hiking trails, interactive exhibits, and aquarium.

Museums & Historic Sites

  • Fort Smith National Historic Site
    Frontier history, Trail of Tears, Judge Parker's courtroom. Free admission, open daily.
  • Fort Smith Museum of History
    Local history exhibits in a 1906 warehouse, plus a working 1920s–40s soda fountain.
  • U.S. Marshals Museum
    National museum documenting the history of the U.S. Marshals Service and federal law enforcement.
  • Fort Smith Regional Art Museum (RAM)
    Contemporary and regional art. Free Saturday make-and-take art projects for all ages.
  • Fort Smith Trolley Museum
    Railroad and streetcar museum with rides on a 1926 electric streetcar.
  • The Clayton House
    1882 Victorian mansion in the Belle Grove Historic District.
  • The Darby House
    Childhood home of WWII General William O. Darby with military memorabilia.
  • Chaffee Crossing Historic District
    Walkable military history district with museums from Fort Chaffee's WWII era, including the Elvis Barbershop Museum.

Libraries

  • Fort Smith Public Library
    Main branch plus Dallas, Miller, and Windsor branches. Summer reading programs, storytimes, STEAM activities, and more.
  • Library's "Things to Do" Page
    The library's own curated list of local attractions and activities.

Arts & Performance

  • Fort Smith Symphony
    Founded 1923, 100+ professional musicians performing six concerts annually.
  • Fort Smith Little Theatre
    Arkansas' oldest all-volunteer community theatre with six annual productions.
  • The Unexpected (Street Art)
    30+ murals and contemporary urban art installations throughout downtown since 2015.

Markets & Community

  • Fort Smith Farmer's Market
    Year-round outdoor market (Tuesdays & Saturdays) with 90+ local growers and merchants.
  • The Bakery District
    Revitalized 1921 historic bakery building, now a downtown community hub.
  • Miss Laura's Visitor Center
    Fort Smith Convention & Visitors Bureau — the only former bordello on the National Register.
